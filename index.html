HOW A BUILD RUNS

Four stages, no theatre

  • 01 Scout — bots survey the spec and split it into buildable pieces.
  • 02 Assemble — the core crew of five builds; big pieces call up reinforcements.
  • 03 Polish — perfectionists stay behind and tweak what is already done.
  • 04 Patrol — finished systems are revisited forever: pixels checked, letters straightened, colours kept honest.
